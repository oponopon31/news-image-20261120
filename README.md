## How it works

A program at the repository root draws the image procedurally and writes it to `/output/entry.png` — no network, fully deterministic, standard library only. Two runs produce a byte-identical PNG (1024×1024).

## Date discipline

Every story on the page happened, or was first reported, on Friday
20 November 2026. The page says so in its DATE CHECK box: the World
Court's ruling on Myanmar and the three-leader declaration in
Shenzhen are Thursday 19 November news and are deliberately left
out, as are Tonga's drought emergency and the migrant boat lost off
Senegal. Not yet shown: BepiColombo, which enters orbit round
Mercury on 21 November (19:24 CET), and any final COP31 text — none
was adopted on 20 November.

Version 2 (21 November 2026) tightened the date rule: it drops
three Thursday stories that v1 carried (Bukele's third-term filing,
the Uganda Ebola rally ban, the Democrats' law-firm query),
corrects the Poland, Moscow, Lebanon, debt-distress and ASX items,
and replaces the gaps with three stories verified for 20 November:
Mamdani's plan to close Rikers Island by 2032, Ebola reported in
