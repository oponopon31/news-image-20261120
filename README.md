# The World in One Day — Friday 20 November 2026

One image summing up the world's news of Friday 20 November 2026:
35 stories in 7 regions (Europe 8, Asia 8, Middle East 3, Africa 5,
Americas 4, Oceania 3, Global 4). Lead stories: Russian missiles hit
three Kyiv bridges, Cyclone Akvan comes ashore in eastern India, and
an AI sell-off sinks Asia's stock markets. A numbered world map ties
stories 1–13 to their places, and a two-column list adds 22 more
stories (14–35), each with its source.

Built for the [make.fun bounty news-image-20261120](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261120).

## How it works

A program at the repository root draws the image procedurally and writes it to `/output/entry.png` — no network, fully deterministic, standard library only. Two runs produce a byte-identical PNG (1024×1024).

## Date discipline

Every story on the page happened, or was first reported, on Friday
20 November 2026. The page says so in its DATE CHECK box: the World
Court's ruling on Myanmar and the three-leader declaration in
Shenzhen are Thursday 19 November news and are deliberately left
out, as are Tonga's drought emergency and the migrant boat lost off
Senegal. Not yet shown: BepiColombo's entry into orbit round Mercury
and any final COP31 text.

The load-bearing claims were re-verified against live reporting on
21 November 2026: Cyclone Akvan's landfall near Gopalpur 07:30–08:30
IST on 20 November, 300,000+ people moved to shelters, no deaths
(IMD); Taiwan's Taiex down 6.23% on 20 November, its worst week
since 1991, Nikkei −5.1%, KOSPI −5.2%, Hang Seng −1.9% (Reuters/AP);
a Russian Kh-101 cruise missile down near Nowy Lipowiec, Poland, on
20 November, with Rzeszów airport briefly suspended (Polish defence
ministry). All pictures are original geometric drawings made by the
program — no photographs, no copied artwork.
